About the clinic

One place for the whole family's dental care.

Western Dental Hospital sits on Bungalow 2, FL 6, Block 4 in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, and brings general dentistry, orthodontics, implants and children's dentistry under one roof.

Appointments are booked on WhatsApp and confirmed by phone with the clinic team. Ask for a checkup or a full treatment plan, and the clinic will explain what happens next before any work starts.
